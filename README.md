# Flourish 🌱 — Grow Into Your Best Self

Flourish is a cute, playful, and interactive habit-tracking web app designed to help you build positive routines, stay consistent, and celebrate your personal growth. Every completed habit earns you XP and petals, helping your very own Growth Garden blossom as you make progress.

✨ **Features**
- 🌷 Create and track daily habits
- 🌱 Watch your interactive Growth Garden grow
- ⭐ Earn XP and petals by completing habits
- 🎨 Customize your experience with theme preferences
- 🔔 Enjoy playful sound effects
- 💾 Automatically save your habits and progress
- 📱 Responsive design for desktop and mobile devices

Flourish turns small daily actions into meaningful progress. Start small, stay consistent, and watch yourself flourish — one habit at a time!

**Built with:** HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
